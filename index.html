Meet the athlete

Brin Hart

Brin Hart is a Class of 2031 student-athlete at Pulaski Academy in Little Rock, Arkansas. A dedicated point guard and shooting guard, Brin brings passion, determination, and a competitive spirit to everything she does.

Faith, family, academics, and basketball are important parts of Brin's life. She loves God and has a strong foundation of faith that keeps her grounded through challenges and victories. Her favorite scripture is Philippians 4:13: "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

Brin has consistently maintained a GPA above 3.5 while balancing her academic responsibilities with school basketball, AAU competition, camps, and training.

Committed to becoming the best version of herself, Brin works with a basketball trainer, a specialized shooting coach, and a stretch and recovery coach. She understands that growth requires discipline, consistency, preparation, and taking care of her body.

Through her experiences with Pulaski Academy and Arkansas Banshees, Brin continues to build confidence, embrace challenges, and grow as both a player and a person.
